Hexapod Underground Utility Tunnel Inspection Robot

My final-year project, built with Naveen M and Vickram R. A six-legged walking robot for inspecting underground utility tunnels: 18 MG996R servos, 3 degrees of freedom per leg, an octagonal body, and an Arduino Mega 2560 coordinating the legs. Phase 1 covers fabrication and coordinated leg movement. Later phases add a camera, remote operation, obstacle detection and autonomous navigation with a Raspberry Pi.
